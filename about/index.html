About

Engineer by training, advisor by profession. I'm Ione, and I help leaders in healthcare and technology turn strategy into results: setting the direction, designing the operating model, and working side by side with teams to put it into practice.

I advise companies and serve on boards. My background in bioengineering and my curiosity about people help me bridge technical depth, business strategy, and the day-to-day reality of making change stick.

I believe education broadens horizons and am fortunate to have had wonderful teachers and opportunities. To share some of this luck, I co-founded PyGirls, a non-profit that teaches girls to code. I enjoy life's wonders through time with friends, the outdoors, making art, and meaningful work.
